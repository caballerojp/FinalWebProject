The City of Pines Awaits You

Baguio City is a nice place if you want to have some cool and nice mountain escape, It is known for its pine forests, fresh air, beautiful scenery and peaceful atmosphere.

As you visit, we encourage you to respect its cleanliness and follow local guidelines.
Be a responsible traveler and be careful for nature, preserve its beauty, and help keep Baguio a place of healing for generations to come.
